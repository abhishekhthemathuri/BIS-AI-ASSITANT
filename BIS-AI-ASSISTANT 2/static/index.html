I manufacture stainless steel water bottles. Which Indian Standard applies and what certification requirements should I know?
Recommended Standard High Relevance

IS 17803 : 2022

Stainless Steel Vacuum Flasks / Insulated Bottles Specification

WHY THIS STANDARD?

Under DPIIT Quality Control Order (QCO), stainless steel vacuum flasks and containers intended for domestic liquids must bear the ISI mark under Scheme-I. Manufacturing without a valid CM/L license is a statutory offense under Section 29 of the BIS Act, 2016.
