# 🏛️ BIS Sahayak AI — Indian Standards & Compliance Intelligence Assistant

> An intelligent, RAG-grounded conversational AI assistant empowering consumers, manufacturers, jewellers, and laboratory auditors with instant, authoritative guidance on Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) regulations, Indian Standards (IS Codes), Quality Control Orders (QCOs), and Hallmarking compliance.

---

##  Problem Statement & Context

Navigating the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) ecosystem can be challenging:
- **Consumers** often struggle to verify authentic 3-symbol Hallmarks, 6-digit HUIDs on gold jewellery, and genuine ISI / CRS marks on electronic goods and helmets.
- **MSMEs and Manufacturers** face complexity identifying mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs), applicable IS codes, lab testing clauses, and documentation for Scheme I (ISI), Scheme II (CRS), or Scheme IV (FMCS).
- **Standards Documents** are dense, clause-heavy, and difficult to query in real time.

**BIS Sahayak AI** bridges this gap by combining **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** with **Google Gemini (Gemini 3.6/3.7 Flash)** to deliver instantaneous, cited, and legally precise answers.

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. ** RAG Grounded with Authentic BIS Knowledge Base**:
   - Covers critical Indian Standards (IS 10500 for Drinking Water, IS 1417 for Gold Hallmarking, IS 13252 for Electronics, IS 1786 for TMT Steel, IS 4151 for Helmets, IS 9873 for Toys, etc.).
   - Extracts exact clause limits, testing parameters, and permissible tolerances.

2. ** Hallmarking & HUID Consumer Verification Guide**:
   - Step-by-step guidance on verifying the 3 authentic gold hallmarks: BIS Triangle Logo, Purity Grade (e.g., `22K916`), and the unique 6-digit alphanumeric HUID via the BIS Care App.

3. ** Scheme Comparison Engine**:
   - Clear breakdowns between **Scheme I (ISI Mark)**, **Scheme II (Compulsory Registration Scheme - CRS)**, and **Scheme IV (Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme - FMCS)**.

4. ** BIS Laboratories & Testing Directory**:
   - Instant lookups for Central Laboratory (CL Sahibabad), Regional Testing Laboratories (WROL Mumbai, SROL Chennai, EROL Kolkata, NROL Mohali), and NABL scopes.

5. ** Multi-Turn Conversational Memory**:
   - Keeps track of conversational context for follow-up questions and compliance cross-examinations.

6. ** Responsive Government-Grade Web UI**:
   - Modern, mobile-responsive layout styled with Tailwind CSS, quick-access topic chips, and real-time Markdown rendering.
